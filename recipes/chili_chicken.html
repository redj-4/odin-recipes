Chili Chicken Recipe

Home

Ingredients

  • Chicken Breast
  • Chili Sauce
  • Soy Sauce
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Green Onions
  • Red Bell Pepper
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized pieces
  2. In a bowl, mix chili sauce, soy sauce, minced garlic, and grated ginger to create the sauce
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a pan over medium-high heat
  4. Add the chicken pieces to the pan and cook until they are browned and cooked through
  5. Add sliced red bell pepper to the pan and cook for a few minutes until they are slightly tender
  6. Pour the sauce over the chicken and bell peppers, stirring to coat everything evenly
  7. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken well
  8. Garnish with chopped green onions before serving
  9. Enjoy your delicious chili chicken!
