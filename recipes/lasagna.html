Lasagna Recipe

Home

Ingredients

  • Lasagna Noodles
  • Ground Beef
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Ricotta Cheese
  • Mozzarella Cheese
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the lasagna noodle
  2. Brown the ground beef with onion and garlic in olive oil
  3. Add tomato sauce to the beef mixture and simmer
  4. In a separate bowl, mix ricotta cheese with salt and pepper
  5. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C)
  6. In a baking dish, layer the ingredients: start with a layer of meat sauce, then noodles, followed by the ricotta mixture, and sprinkle mozzarella and parmesan cheese on top. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a layer of cheese on top.
  7. Cover the dish with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes
  8. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown
  9. Let the lasagna cool for a few minutes before serving
  10. Voila! You're done, enjoy the meal
